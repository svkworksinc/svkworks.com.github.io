Why Quality Connectors?

The Right Connector for Every Circuit

Connectors are the most vulnerable part of any wiring harness. Heat cycling, vibration, and moisture ingress cause contact resistance that leads to misfires, sensor errors, and intermittent gremlins. We stock the same connectors used in every SVK Works harness.

OEM Toyota Kits

Factory-spec connectors for 2JZ, 1JZ, 7M-GTE, and 1UZ-FE. Correct fit, correct retention, correct sealing for each application.

🛡️

Deutsch DT Series

Motorsport-grade sealed connectors with individual wire seals and wedge-lock retention. The industry standard for performance wiring.

Autosport Mil-Spec

Quick-disconnect firewall connectors for clean engine removal. Used in Formula racing and top-level motorsport applications worldwide.
