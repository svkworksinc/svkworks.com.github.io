Lexus SC300 / SC400 Wiring Diagrams (1992–1998)

Complete factory OEM electrical wiring diagrams for every model year of the Lexus SC300 (2JZ-GE) and SC400 (1UZ-FE) Z30 platform. Essential for standalone builds, 2JZ swap wiring, and 1UZ diagnostic work.

The Lexus SC300 and SC400 (Z30 chassis) were produced from 1992 to 2000. The SC300 uses the 2JZ-GE inline-six while the SC400 uses the 1UZ-FE V8 — both popular platforms for standalone ECU swaps and engine conversions. These factory EWDs cover all chassis wiring, engine circuits, ABS, traction control, A/C, and body electrics.
