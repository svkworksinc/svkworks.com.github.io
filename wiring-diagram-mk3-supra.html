Toyota Supra MK3 (MA70 / JZA70) Wiring Diagrams

Factory OEM electrical wiring diagrams for the Toyota Supra MK3. Covers 1987 and 1990 model years — essential for 7M-GTE diagnosis, 2JZ/1JZ swap wiring, and standalone ECU builds.

The Toyota Supra MK3 (MA70 / JZA70) was produced from 1986 to 1992. The 7M-GTE was the factory turbocharged engine in early cars, while later Japanese models used the 1JZ-GTE. A very popular swap is the 2JZ engine into the MK3 chassis — these factory EWDs are the starting point for any serious standalone harness build or chassis wiring repair.
