Why Delete Power Steering

Cleaner Bay. Less Parasitic Drag.

The 2JZ power steering pump is a constant hydraulic load on the engine. On a track build or a high-power swap where you're running a standalone setup, deleting it eliminates a common leak point, frees up a few horsepower, and results in a much cleaner engine bay. This kit gives you a complete, belt-driven solution — no fabrication, no leftover holes, and your A/C stays cold.
