Service 03

Annual Harness Health Check

Race cars and track builds take a beating. Send your SVK harness in once a year — we inspect every connector, test critical circuits, re-loom any wear points, and return it with a clean bill of health and a new inspection sticker.

What's Checked

  • All connector retaining clips
  • Wire chafing at bulkhead pass-throughs
  • ECU connector pin tension
  • Ground circuit resistance
  • Injector and coil circuits
  • Battery and main power loom

Mail-In Process

How to Send Your Harness

The full process from booking to getting your harness back.

1

Book Your Service Online

Click the relevant button above, fill out the short form, and select your service type. You'll receive a confirmation email within one business day.

2

Package Your Harness

Print and include the booking confirmation form inside the box. Coil the harness loosely, wrap with bubble wrap, and use a rigid box. Label clearly with your booking reference.

3

Ship to Our Facility

Use any carrier. We recommend insuring the shipment for the harness replacement value. A prepaid return label option is available at checkout for an additional fee.

4

We'll email you when your harness arrives and again when work is complete. For repairs, you'll receive a quote before we touch anything. Service time starts from receipt, not booking.

5

Receive Your Harness + Report

Your harness ships back with your digital report or inspection documentation. PDF reports are also emailed directly so you always have a copy on file.

FAQ

Common Questions

Do you accept harnesses from any manufacturer?

The Diagnostic Inspection and Repair services accept any harness regardless of brand or origin. The Annual Inspection is exclusively for SVK Works harnesses — use the Repair service for other brands.

Should I insure my shipment?

Yes. We strongly recommend insuring your harness for its replacement value. SVK Works is not responsible for loss or damage during transit to our facility. We take full responsibility once it arrives.

Is there a warranty on repair work?

Yes. All repair and modification work is covered by a 12-month workmanship warranty. If a repair we performed fails within that period due to our workmanship, we'll fix it at no charge (you cover return shipping).

How long does return shipping take?

We ship returns via ground courier by default (3–5 business days in the continental US). Expedited return shipping is available. International returns are shipped via tracked air freight.

Can I get a diagnostic inspection and then decide on repair separately?

Absolutely. This is a common workflow — book the Diagnostic Inspection first, receive the report, then decide whether to proceed with repairs and at what scope. The $175 diagnostic fee applies regardless of whether you proceed with repair. If you do book a repair within 30 days of receiving your diagnostic report, we credit the diagnostic fee toward the repair cost.

Not sure which service you need?

Describe your issue and we'll recommend the right option — no obligation. Most issues are easy to triage over email before you ship anything.
