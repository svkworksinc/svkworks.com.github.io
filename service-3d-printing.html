3D Part Design & Printing

Quote-Based

Sometimes the right part doesn't exist — so we make it. SVK Works designs and prints custom parts to support harness builds and engine bay fitment: relay boxes, ECU mounting brackets, connector housings, cable management clips, and more. Parts designed to fit your specific application and printed in high-strength materials.

  • Custom ECU and relay box brackets
  • Firewall pass-through plates and grommets
  • Sensor adapters and mount brackets
  • Cable management and harness retention clips
  • High-temperature materials available (ASA, PETG, Nylon)
  • Design and print included — or print from your file

Design or Print-Only

We offer full design-and-print service where we CAD model the part from your measurements or photos and print it. Alternatively, if you already have an STL or STEP file, we can print it directly. Contact us with your requirement and we'll advise on the best material and approach for your application.
