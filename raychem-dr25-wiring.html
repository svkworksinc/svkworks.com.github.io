Why DR-25?

Raychem DR-25 is an irradiated polyolefin heat-shrink tubing manufactured by TE Connectivity (formerly Raychem). It is specified in aerospace and military wiring standards for its resistance to extreme temperatures, chemicals, abrasion, and flame. It's also widely used in professional motorsport for its durability in high-heat environments.
Standard PVC or polyolefin heat shrink is rated to around 85–105°C and offers minimal chemical resistance. DR-25 is rated to 135°C continuous with a 175°C peak, is self-extinguishing, and resists virtually all automotive fluids including fuels, oils, and coolant. In a high-boost or forced induction environment, the difference in longevity is significant.
