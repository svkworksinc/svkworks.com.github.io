How It Works

A MIL-Spec Autosport connector is a multi-pin circular connector used in motorsport and military applications. It provides a single, sealed disconnect point between the engine harness and the chassis harness, allowing the entire engine loom to be separated cleanly at the firewall.
It's not required — SVK harnesses work perfectly without it. However, if you plan on pulling the engine regularly (track car, ongoing build, or frequent maintenance), the quick-disconnect saves significant time. It also gives the engine bay a clean, professional finish.
The metal mounting plate bolts to the firewall and holds the Autosport connector in place. It provides a secure, vibration-resistant mount point and creates a sealed pass-through between the engine bay and interior. The plate is CNC-cut for a precision fit.
Select "Yes" under the Milspec Autosport Connector option in the harness build calculator on your vehicle's page. The connector is integrated into the harness during the build — it's not a separate add-on kit. You can also mention it in the notes when emailing your build request.
The MIL-Spec connector option is available for all SVK harness platforms: MK4 Supra (JZA80), MK3 Supra (MA70), and Lexus SC300/SC400/Soarer (Z30).
