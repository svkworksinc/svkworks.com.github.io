Why Tefzel?

Tefzel is the brand name for ETFE (ethylene tetrafluoroethylene) insulated wire manufactured by DuPont. It is specified under MIL-W-22759 for aerospace and military applications. The ETFE insulation provides a unique combination of high temperature rating, chemical resistance, thin wall construction, and abrasion resistance that no other common automotive wire insulation can match.
TXL (cross-linked polyethylene) wire is rated to 125°C and is SVK's standard wire — already a step above typical automotive wiring. Tefzel wire is rated to 150°C, has a thinner insulation wall (resulting in a smaller, lighter harness), and substantially better chemical and abrasion resistance. For race and high-output builds, Tefzel is the next level up.
