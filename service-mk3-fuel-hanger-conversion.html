MK3 Supra Fuel Pump Hanger Conversion

8AN feed & 6AN return — custom sizes available on request

Starting ~$250 Quote required

If you're upgrading the fuel system on your MK3 Supra, the factory plastic hanger nipples are the first thing that needs to go. They're a liability on a high-pressure, high-flow setup and don't give you a proper AN fitting connection. We modify your existing hanger by removing those nipples and welding in proper AN fittings — 8AN on the feed side and 6AN on the return side as standard — so you can run clean braided lines all the way to the rail.

You ship us your hanger, we do the work, and ship it back. Clean welds, properly sized fittings, and no more worrying about a plastic barb letting go under pressure. If your build calls for different fitting sizes, just ask — we can accommodate custom configurations.

Why Modify

The Right Fittings. The Right Way.

The factory MK3 Supra hanger uses push-on plastic nipples that were designed for the stock fuel system — not the higher pressures and flow rates that come with a pump upgrade. Welded AN fittings eliminate a failure point and give you a clean, permanent, industry-standard connection that will outlast any hose you put on it.
