Frequently Asked Questions

What vehicles does this service cover?

This repair covers the 1992–2000 Lexus SC300 (JZZ30), SC400 (UZZ30), and Toyota Soarer Z30 (both JZZ30 and UZZ30). All model years in this Z30 generation use the same climate control head unit with identical LCD and backlight design.

What symptoms indicate my climate control LCD needs repair?

Common failures include a completely black (dead) display, segments that are barely visible or invisible, half the display not showing, flickering readout under temperature changes, or backlighting that no longer comes on. These are almost always caused by failed electrolytic capacitors and a faulty LCD ribbon connection — the climate control module itself is rarely the culprit.

What does the base $329 price include?

The base service includes full disassembly, replacement of all aging electrolytic capacitors, LCD ribbon re-flow and resolder, cleaning, function test, reassembly, and return shipping back to you (contiguous US). Backlight color changes for the screen, buttons, and indicators are included at no extra charge. A new LCD panel is optional (+$45) for units with cracked or pixel-damaged screens.

Do I need to send anything other than the climate control unit?

No — send just the climate control head unit (the panel you remove from the center dash). You do not need to include wiring harnesses, the blower resistor, or any other HVAC components. Wrap the unit in bubble wrap and ship it in a padded box or envelope using a tracked service.

What color options are available?

We offer OEM Amber (factory stock — no change), White, Blue, Green, Red, and Purple for the screen display segments, button backlighting, and indicator LEDs above the buttons. Each can be set independently, so you can mix and match to suit your interior.

When does the repair timer start?

Turnaround begins the next business day after we physically receive your unit. Standard service is 7–10 business days from receipt. Expedited service (2–4 business days) is available for $149 — roughly half what other providers charge for rush service.

Is return shipping really included?

Yes — return shipping back to you within the contiguous US is included in the service price. You are responsible for getting your unit to us; we'll get it back to you. For international orders, contact us before ordering for a return shipping quote.

What warranty is included?

All repairs carry a 1-year workmanship warranty. If the same fault recurs within 12 months of your repair date, ship it back and we'll fix it at no charge (you cover inbound shipping). This does not cover new failures unrelated to the original repair or physical damage.

Building or swapping your SC300 / SC400?

SVK Works specializes in the Z30 platform. Beyond climate control repair, we build custom standalone wiring harnesses for 1JZ and 2JZ swaps, source hard-to-find OEM connectors, and offer wiring repair for corroded or damaged factory harnesses.
