Custom Wiring Harness Design

Quote-Based

Not every build fits a standard harness. If you have a unique swap, unusual platform, or non-standard ECU, SVK Works builds fully custom harnesses from scratch — designed around your exact requirements, routed for your specific chassis, and built to last.

  • Any engine and ECU combination supported
  • Custom routing designed for your chassis
  • New wire, OEM-spec connectors, motorsport materials
  • All circuits tested for continuity before shipping
  • Optional MILSpec connector upgrade available
  • Turn-key engine start guarantee

The Process

Start by contacting us with the details of your build: engine, ECU, chassis, and any special requirements. We'll review the scope, ask clarifying questions, and provide a quote. Once confirmed, we design, build, and test the harness before shipping it to you or arranging local fitment.

Our catalog harnesses (MK4 Supra, MK3 Supra, SC300/SC400) are pre-engineered for those specific platforms with known variables. A custom harness is for anything outside those platforms or with significant deviations — different engine, different chassis, unusual ECU, or exotic application like race cars and purpose-built vehicles.
