Frequently Asked Questions

What connector does this firewall plate fit?

This plate is designed specifically for the 61-pin Autosport/Milspec circular bulkhead connector, the standard used in mil-spec and motorsport harness builds.

Is the 61-pin connector included?

The plate is sold separately from the connector. The 61-pin Autosport/Milspec connector is available — contact us for current pricing and availability.

Why use a bulkhead connector instead of routing wires directly through the firewall?

A bulkhead connector gives you a single clean disconnect point between the engine harness and cabin harness. Engine pulls become far simpler, chafing through bare firewall holes is eliminated, and the result looks professional rather than cobbled together. It's the standard on every SVK Works mil-spec build.

Does this require cutting the firewall?

Yes — the plate mounts over an opening in the firewall sized for the 61-pin bulkhead connector. Contact us if you need guidance on placement and sizing for your specific build.
