How the Waitlist Works

Reserve now, spec later. We handle the rest.

01

Reserve Your Slot

Pick your preferred build month and submit a small deposit. No need to have your full spec finalized yet.

$100 deposit
02

We Contact You 2 Weeks Before

Two weeks before your build month begins, we'll reach out to confirm your exact harness specification and options.

03

Pay the Build Balance

Once your spec is confirmed, the remaining balance is due. Your $100 deposit is applied — you only pay the difference.

04

Your Harness Ships

We build your harness during your reserved slot and ship it on schedule. Track your order in your SVK account.
