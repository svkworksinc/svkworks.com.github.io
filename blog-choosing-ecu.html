Choosing the Right ECU for Your 2JZ Build

Haltech, Link, AEM, Motec — the options are overwhelming. Here's our breakdown of the most popular ECU choices for 2JZ-powered builds.

← Back to Blog

Choosing an ECU is one of the most important decisions in your build. Each platform has strengths depending on your goals — budget, tuning support, I/O requirements, and how serious you are about data logging will all influence the right choice for you.

Haltech Elite Series

User-friendly software, excellent support community, and great I/O. The Haltech Elite 2500 is a favorite for street and mild track builds. Their PC-Link software has a relatively gentle learning curve and Haltech's customer support is responsive. If you're new to standalone ECU tuning, Haltech is a comfortable starting point.

Link G4X / G4+

Powerful and cost-effective. The Link G4X offers exceptional value with outstanding logging and tuning features. Extremely popular in the drift community. PCLink (their tuning software) is more complex than Haltech's interface but offers deeper control. A favorite for both street builds and competition-level setups.

AEM Infinity

Professional-grade with extensive I/O. The AEM Infinity is preferred by many professional tuners for its flexibility and reliability. Strong in the time attack and road race world. Requires more tuning experience to get the most out of it, but rewards that investment.

Motec M1

Top-tier motorsport ECU. Unmatched data logging and strategy capabilities. Best for serious competition builds where data analysis and advanced engine management strategies matter. The M1 is significantly more expensive and requires specialized knowledge to tune, but there is no better option for high-level motorsport applications.
