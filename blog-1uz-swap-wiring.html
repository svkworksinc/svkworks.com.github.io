1UZ-FE Swap Wiring: What You Need to Know

The 1UZ-FE is a proven V8 swap candidate. Here's a breakdown of the wiring challenges and how a purpose-built harness makes the install clean and reliable.

← Back to Blog

The Toyota/Lexus 1UZ-FE is one of the most popular V8 swap engines for good reason — it's compact, reliable, relatively affordable, and makes great power with modern tuning. Whether you're dropping it into an S-chassis, a truck, or keeping it in a Lexus with a manual transmission swap, the wiring can be the most complex part of the job.

VVT-i vs. Non-VVT-i: It Matters

The 1UZ came in two main variants — the earlier non-VVT-i (1990–1997) and the later VVT-i (1998–2001). The VVT-i version is more desirable for power potential, but it adds complexity to the wiring. The VVT-i system requires additional control circuits and different cam sensor wiring compared to the earlier motors.

Key Wiring Challenges

  • Sequential fuel injection: The 1UZ uses a sequential injection system with two ECU connectors and a significant number of pins. A clean standalone harness simplifies this dramatically.
  • Coil-on-plug ignition: Eight individual coils, each needing a dedicated ignition output from the ECU.
  • TRAC/VSC systems: The factory ABS, traction control, and stability control systems are deeply integrated into the OEM wiring. A standalone harness eliminates these unless you specifically need them.
  • Transmission integration: Whether you're running the factory A650E automatic or a CD009, R154, or other manual swap, the harness needs to account for your specific transmission.

The OEM Harness Route

Some builders attempt to use the factory 1UZ harness, cutting and extending to fit the swap vehicle. This results in a harness designed around a Lexus LS400 or SC400 platform being crammed into a completely different chassis with different sensor locations, different accessories, and different electrical architecture. The factory harness is massive — full of unused circuits for luxury features that have no place in your build.
