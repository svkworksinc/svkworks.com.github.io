Toyota Supra MK4 (JZA80) Wiring Diagrams

Factory OEM electrical wiring diagrams for the 1993–1998 Toyota Supra JZA80. Covers all domestic and J-spec variants — essential for 2JZ-GTE, 2JZ-GE, and 1JZ engine swaps.

The Toyota Supra JZA80 (MK4) was produced from 1993 to 2002, with US-spec models running 1993–1998. Whether you're building a standalone 2JZ-GTE harness, doing a 1JZ or 1UZ swap, or simply tracing a fault, these factory EWDs cover every circuit in the car — engine, chassis, body, and A/C. The J-spec workshop manual is particularly thorough and includes pinouts, relay diagrams, and connector locations not found in the USDM EWD.
