Return Policy

Due to the custom nature of our wiring harnesses, we cannot accept returns on custom orders. Each harness is built specifically for your engine, chassis, and ECU combination.

If you receive a harness with a defect or error (incorrect pinout, wrong connector, damaged wire), contact us immediately. We will repair or rebuild the harness at no additional cost.
