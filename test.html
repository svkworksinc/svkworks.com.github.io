TXL vs Tefzel Wire

Choose the right option for your harness build

TXL (Automotive Grade)

  • ✅ OEM-standard automotive wire
  • ✅ Flexible & easy to route
  • ✅ Rated -40°C to +125°C
  • ✅ Affordable & reliable
  • ⚠️ Less chemical/heat resistant
  • ⚠️ Not motorsport-grade

Best for: Street cars & OEM+ builds

Tefzel (Mil-Spec)

  • ✅ Aerospace / motorsport grade
  • ✅ Extreme chemical & abrasion resistance
  • ✅ Rated -65°C to +150°C (sometimes 200°C)
  • ✅ Lightweight & thin insulation
  • ⚠️ Stiffer, harder to route
  • ⚠️ Higher cost, requires pro tooling

Best for: Race cars & high-performance builds
