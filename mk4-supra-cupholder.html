Frequently Asked Questions

Yes. This cupholder is designed specifically for the JZA80 MK4 Supra (1993–2002). It mounts in the center console area and requires no modification to the interior. RHD and LHD variants are the same fitment.
The cupholder supports containers up to 90mm in diameter — this covers standard 330ml / 12oz cans, 500ml water bottles, and most travel mugs. Slim/tapered containers seat best.
Car interiors get hot — especially on summer days. PLA starts to soften around 60°C, which is easily reached inside a parked car. PETG handles temperatures up to 80°C+ without deforming, making it the right choice for interior parts. It's also more impact-resistant than PLA.
The cupholder clips or sits into the existing center console area — no drilling, no glue, no permanent changes. It's designed to be removable so you can take it out when not needed.
