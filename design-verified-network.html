FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

? How do I get verified?

Submit your application using the form above. Our team will review your shop's background, experience with JDM platforms, and wiring harness work. If approved, you'll be contacted with next steps and onboarding materials.

? Is there a fee to join?

No. The SVK Verified Installer program is completely free to join. There are no membership fees, monthly charges, or required purchase minimums. We want great installers in the network, full stop.

? What are the requirements?

Shops must have demonstrable experience with standalone ECU installations and JDM engine management wiring. A professional shop space and willingness to follow SVK installation guidelines are required. We may request photos or references.

? How long does approval take?

Most applications are reviewed within 5–7 business days. If we have questions, a member of the SVK Works team will reach out directly. Once approved, your shop is listed in the directory within 48 hours.
