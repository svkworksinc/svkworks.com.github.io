SVK Works Return & Refund Policy

At SVK Works, due to the nature of our products, **all returns are strictly limited**. Custom-built harnesses and specialized engine wiring are non-returnable and cannot be refunded or exchanged.

Eligibility for Returns

  • Only defective or incorrect items may be eligible for return.
  • Returns must be requested within 14 days of delivery.
  • Items must be unused, unmodified, and shipped in original packaging.
  • Proof of defect must be provided with photos or videos.

Non-Returnable Items

  • All custom harnesses built for specific engines, ECUs, or configurations
  • Products altered, installed incorrectly, or damaged due to misuse
  • Any harness that has been cut, spliced, or modified in any way

Return Process

  1. Email support@svkworks.com with order number and issue.
  2. Include photos of the defect or damage.
  3. If approved, you will receive a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) and instructions.
  4. Ship the item with the RMA clearly marked. Returns sent without authorization will not be accepted.

Refunds

  • Refunds for approved returns will be issued to the original payment method.
  • Refunds do not include shipping or handling fees.
  • Refund processing time is 5–7 business days after receipt and inspection.
  • Refunds will be issued at 50% of total payment.

Contact

Questions regarding returns? Contact: support@svkworks.com
