Service Options

Harness Conversion

Common for: MK3 Supra, S13/S14, E30, etc.

This service is for those using the stock ECU that came with their engine. We take the body-side connectors from your original car and professionally pin them into the donor engine harness.

  • Retains factory speedometer and tachometer signals.
  • Properly routes check engine light and diagnostic pins.
  • Ensures your stock ignition key triggers the starter and EFI relays.

Harness Extension

Essential for JDM-to-USDM Swaps

Stock JDM harnesses are designed for Right-Hand Drive cars, where the ECU sits on the opposite side. When putting these into a Left-Hand Drive chassis, the harness is physically too short to reach the passenger kick panel.

  • Avoids "stretching" the harness across the engine bay.
  • Each wire is extended using automotive-grade TXL wire.
  • Finished in high-temp loom to match the factory look.
