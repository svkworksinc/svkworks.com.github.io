SC400 Manual Swap: Wiring the CD009 Conversion

Swapping a Nissan 350Z CD009 6-speed into the SC400 is one of the cleanest manual conversions available. Here's everything you need to know about the wiring side.

← Back to Blog

The Lexus SC400 is one of the best platforms for a manual transmission conversion — a smooth 1UZ-FE V8, a well-balanced chassis, and the CD009 6-speed from the Nissan 350Z bolts in with an adapter plate. But the wiring requires careful attention.

What Changes on the Wiring Side

  • Neutral Safety Switch: The automatic trans has a range switch that the ECU monitors. With a manual, you'll need to wire in a simple clutch switch or jumper the circuit so the car starts in gear.
  • VSS (Vehicle Speed Sensor): The CD009 has its own VSS output. You'll need to match the signal to what your ECU and gauge cluster expect — often a simple adapter or tuning table adjustment.
  • Reverse Lights: Wire the reverse light switch from the CD009 into the SC chassis harness for full functionality.
  • Cruise Control: OEM cruise control will need to be re-configured or deleted, as the auto trans throttle strategy changes with a manual.

Running a Standalone ECU

If you're doing a full standalone ECU conversion alongside the manual swap — which we strongly recommend — many of these integration challenges simplify significantly. A standalone ECU gives you full control over start enable strategy, VSS calibration, and eliminates the OEM cruise control complexity entirely.
