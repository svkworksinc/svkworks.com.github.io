Just tell us your target chassis when you submit your request and we'll spec the lead lengths accordingly.
We build for all major standalone ECUs including Haltech Elite, Link G4X, MaxxECU, ECUMaster EMU Black / Pro, AEM Infinity, Motec M1, FuelTech FT550, and more. If your ECU isn't listed in the form, add it in the notes field and we'll accommodate it.
Yes. The 2JZ-GE Non-VVTi relies on a distributor for ignition timing and does not have coil-on-plug. Running a standalone ECU requires converting to direct ignition via a cam trigger kit (Kelford and similar suppliers offer these). We wire the harness for coil-on-plug but the trigger kit needs to be on the engine before we build.
All SVK harnesses are built new from scratch using high-temperature TXL cross-linked polyethylene wire and OEM-style Toyota / Sumitomo connectors as standard. Raychem Spec 44 motorsport-grade wire is available as an upgrade for race builds.
